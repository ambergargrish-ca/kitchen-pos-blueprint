# Regions and tax

A region sets: brand name (optional), currency, tax model, default rates, time zone, wording, phone format, postcode format, gift card expiry, payment providers, policies, overtime defaults, and which provinces or states are offered.

## New Zealand (case study)

NZD, 15% GST included in prices (tax = total x 15/115), Pacific/Auckland, Stripe or Windcave, gift cards valid at least 3 years, NZ Privacy Act 2020, Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act, no statutory weekly overtime threshold. Card surcharges: a bill to ban them on in-person payments had not passed as of 21 November 2026; keep the switch per region (REG-06) and check with a lawyer.

## Canada (case study)

CAD, prices before tax, tax added at checkout by province, America/Toronto by default, Stripe (others once integrated), gift cards with no expiry (most provinces ban it), PIPEDA, CASL.

| Province | Default combined rate on restaurant food |
|---|---|
| ON | 13% HST (Ontario prepared-food rebate: 5% only at $4.00 or less, no alcohol) |
| NS | 14% HST |
| NB, NL, PE | 15% HST |
| BC, AB | 5% GST (BC PST does not apply to restaurant food; it does to alcohol) |
| SK | 11% (GST + PST) |
| MB | 12% (GST + RST) |
| Territories | 5% GST |
| QC | Not offered until GST + 9.975% QST (two lines) and French-language service are built |

Defaults only. Verify current rates and item rules with an accountant before taking paying customers, and let each business edit its rate.

## Checklist for a new region

1. Tax: inclusive or exclusive, components, rounding rule, special items, small-bill rebates, receipt requirements (tax number, line layout).
2. Money: currency, formatting, smallest coin (cash rounding where coins were dropped).
3. Time: zones, DST, business-day cutoff norms.
4. Phones and postcodes: formats, which numbers you text.
5. Payments: providers available, surcharge law, Connect availability.
6. Consumer law: gift card expiry, refund rights, consent for marketing.
7. Privacy: law, data residency, breach notification.
8. Employment: overtime threshold, breaks, tip rules.
9. Language: spelling, mandatory languages.
10. Policies: privacy policy and terms checked by a local lawyer.

Until all ten are done, the region is not offered.
